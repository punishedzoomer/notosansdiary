---

## Why a digital garden?

Social media networks have manipulated us into believing that the age of personal websites is dead. I mean nowadays we spend most of our lives consuming someone else's Instagram or Tiktok content that somehow we have deluded ourselves that those are our internet safe spaces. 
I have always felt some sort of pain because i didn't have a personal website so hopefully this garden cures me.
