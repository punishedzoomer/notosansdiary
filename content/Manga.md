Lately I have been reading lots of mangas for free and without downloading anything - well except this app. 

[Aidoku](https://aidoku.app/) is a foss app developed by [skitty](https://skitty.xyz/) . I found it because I wanted to start reading berserk then I got ragebaited by all the manga reading websites. Eventually I decided that I won't be dealing with all the ads anymore ; That's how I found this gem.
